    How to play here

    Globe
    Drag to rotate · wheel / pinch to zoom · two-finger or right-drag to pan · double-click (or H) to recentre.
    Your turn
    Glowing cards in your hand are playable — click one to read it and play it. Standard projects, card actions, milestones and awards are on the right.
    Placing tiles
    Candidate hexes glow gold. Hover to preview the tile and see what the space gives you.
    Undo
    Undo anything until a card is revealed. When your turn ends you get Confirm turn — nothing reaches TFMBot until you confirm. Ctrl+Z undo · Enter confirm · Esc cancel.
    Opponent
    Hover (or hold) a player board to see their tableau. The feed on the left shows each TFMBot move and what else it considered; the top bar shows its estimate of your win chance.
    Tracks
    Temperature: heat-production bonuses at −24 and −20, an ocean at 0 °C. Oxygen: +1 temperature at 8%. Oceans: 9 slots.
    💡 Hint
    Asks TFMBot what it would play in your position, with the search share of its top options.
